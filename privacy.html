Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

MINERVA Uploader is an internal tool used only by MINERVA AI Studios to upload videos it has produced to its own YouTube channels.

What we access

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the Google OAuth scope youtube.upload solely to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own channels.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or other users.
  • We do not read, modify or delete any other YouTube or Google account data.
  • We do not sell or transfer any data to third parties.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API usage is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact MINERVA AI Studios via the contact details on our YouTube channels.
